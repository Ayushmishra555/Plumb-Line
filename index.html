How to play

  • 1Tap to drop the swinging floor.
  • 2The centre of the new floor must sit over the floor below. Miss and you lose a hard hat.
  • 3The whole stack is weighed on every joint. When the lean gauge passes its limit, everything falls.
  • 4Steel floors are heavy, timber floors are light. Wind picks up as you climb.
  • 5Three perfect drops in a row straighten the tower. Guide off scores 1.5x.
